Aboutنبذة عني

A career built on the ground, in the boardroom, and in court.مسيرة مهنية بين الميدان وقاعات مجالس الإدارة والمحاكم

Ashraf Elwan is a senior Egyptian lawyer and international legal consultant with more than three decades of practice before Egypt's Appellate and Cassation Courts. His career began inside the energy sector itself — as Deputy Licensing Manager at Mobil Egypt (1997–2003), where he led the asset-title audit of filling-station real estate ahead of the global Mobil–Exxon merger — before he was appointed Legal Counsel and Representative for Total Marketing Egypt (now TotalEnergies Marketing Egypt) in 2003, a role he has held for over twenty years.

Alongside his corporate work, he has run his own practice since 1991, advising trading, technology, and manufacturing companies, sitting on Egypt's Tax Legislation Committee, and defending clients in complex criminal matters. He trains fuel-station and depot personnel on hazardous-materials handling and emergency response, and has completed specialized programs in anti-corruption, anti-money laundering, and data protection.
